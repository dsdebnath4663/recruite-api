### **What is a Data Sharing Rule? **

A **Data Sharing Rule** is a way to control **who can access what data** within an organization. It allows organizations
to share specific data (like candidate details, job openings, or client records) with selected users, roles, or groups,
beyond the default permissions set for the system.

---

### **Why Do We Need Data Sharing Rules?**

By default, sensitive data is kept **private** (only visible to the person who owns it and their manager). However,
there are cases where **collaboration** is needed, and data must be shared with:

- **Teams working together** on a project.
- **Recruiters** needing to access job openings created by other recruiters.
- **Managers** who need an overview of their team's activity.

Data Sharing Rules extend the visibility of such records **without compromising security**.

---

### **Key Scenarios for Data Sharing Rules**

1. **A Hiring Manager Needs Access to Candidates Shared by Recruiters**
    - A recruiter owns a candidate record.
    - The Hiring Manager needs to review those candidates, but default permissions do not allow it.
    - A Data Sharing Rule grants the Hiring Manager **Read/Write** access to those candidate records.

2. **A Senior Manager Needs Access to All Job Openings Created by Recruiters**
    - By default, recruiters can only see their own job openings.
    - A Data Sharing Rule allows senior managers to view or modify all job openings for better oversight.

3. **Team Collaboration on Interviews**
    - Interviews are owned by one user.
    - A Data Sharing Rule enables all team members to **view and update** interview schedules.

---

### **How Do Data Sharing Rules Work?**

1. **Default Permissions**:
    - Every record starts as **private**.
    - Only the owner and their manager can access it.

2. **Extend Access with Sharing Rules**:
    - You define **who** can access the data (e.g., a group, role, or specific user).
    - You define **what type of access** they get:
        - **Read-Only**: They can only see the data.
        - **Read/Write**: They can see and edit the data.
        - **Read/Write/Delete**: They can see, edit, and delete the data.

3. **Controlled Visibility**:
    - Sharing rules cannot **restrict** access; they can only **extend** it.
    - For example, if everyone already has **Read/Write** access, you cannot remove that with a sharing rule.

---

### **Layman Examples**

- **Without Data Sharing Rules**:
    - You own a document on your computer. Only you can see and edit it.
    - Your boss can see it because they oversee your work.

- **With Data Sharing Rules**:
    - You decide to share the document with your colleague.
    - You give them permission to either:
        - Just **view** the document (Read-Only).
        - Make **changes** to the document (Read/Write).
        - Even **delete** the document (Read/Write/Delete).

---

### **Benefits of Data Sharing Rules**

1. **Controlled Collaboration**:
    - Share data only with the necessary people.
    - Prevent unauthorized access or modifications.

2. **Enhanced Security**:
    - Keep sensitive data private unless sharing is explicitly needed.

3. **Increased Productivity**:
    - Allow teams to work together efficiently by sharing necessary data.

4. **Scalability**:
    - Manage data access seamlessly as your organization grows.

---

### **Key Takeaways**

- **Data Sharing Rules** allow you to share data in a **controlled and secure manner**.
- They **extend access**, not restrict it.
- They ensure **teams can collaborate** effectively while keeping sensitive data private.
- Different levels of access (**Read, Write, Delete**) let you fine-tune who can do what with the shared data.
